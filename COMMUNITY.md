## Community Values

### Respect

We believe in treating all community members with respect and dignity. Everyone deserves to be heard and valued, regardless of their background or experience level.

### Inclusivity

Our community welcomes people from all backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. We celebrate diversity and believe it makes our community stronger.

### Collaboration

We succeed together. We encourage members to share knowledge, help each other, and collaborate on solving problems.

### Transparency

We believe in open and honest communication. We share our plans, decisions, and challenges with the community.

### Learning

We're all here to learn. We encourage questions, experimentation, and continuous improvement.

## Code of Conduct

### Expected Behavior

**Be Respectful**
- Treat others with kindness and respect
- Listen actively to different perspectives
- Disagree constructively
- Avoid personal attacks

**Be Inclusive**
- Welcome newcomers
- Help others learn
- Celebrate diversity
- Create safe spaces

**Be Honest**
- Share accurate information
- Admit mistakes
- Give credit to others
- Disclose conflicts of interest

**Be Helpful**
- Answer questions patiently
- Share knowledge generously
- Help others succeed
- Support community members

**Be Professional**
- Use appropriate language
- Stay on topic
- Respect others' time
- Follow community guidelines

### Unacceptable Behavior

**Harassment**
- Offensive comments about identity
- Threats or intimidation
- Unwanted advances
- Sustained disruption

**Discrimination**
- Prejudice based on identity
- Exclusionary behavior
- Stereotyping
- Bias-based comments

**Abuse**
- Personal attacks
- Insults or name-calling
- Trolling or bad faith
- Spam or self-promotion

**Misinformation**
- Spreading false information
- Scams or fraud
- Misleading advice
- Pump and dump schemes

**Inappropriate Content**
- Explicit or sexual content
- Violence or gore
- Hate speech
- Illegal content

## Conflict Resolution

### If You Have a Conflict

**Step 1: Direct Communication**
- Talk to the person involved
- Be respectful and clear
- Try to understand their perspective
- Seek common ground

**Step 2: Community Mediation**
- Ask a community leader for help
- Explain the situation
- Provide context
- Listen to both sides

**Step 3: Escalation**
- Contact project lead
- Provide documentation
- Explain impact
- Request resolution

**Step 4: Resolution**
- Accept decision
- Move forward
- Learn from experience
- Support community
