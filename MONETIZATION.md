### Payment Providers

**Primary**: Stripe
- Supports 135+ currencies
- Low fees (2.9% + $0.30)
- Good developer experience
- Excellent support

**Alternative**: PayPal
- Wide adoption
- Multiple payment methods
- Competitive fees

### Billing

**Cycle**: Monthly or annual

**Renewal**: Automatic recurring
- Send reminder 7 days before
- Retry failed payments
- Allow manual renewal

**Cancellation**: Easy cancellation
- No lock-in period
- Pro-rata refunds
- Export data on cancellation
