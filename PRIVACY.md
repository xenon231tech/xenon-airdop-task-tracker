# Privacy Policy

## Overview

XENON AIRDROP ULTIMATE is committed to protecting your privacy. This document explains how we handle your data.

## Data Collection

### What We Collect

**Minimal Data Collection**:
- We collect minimal data to provide our service
- Most data stays on your device
- No personal information required

**On Your Device**:
- Task data
- User preferences
- Theme settings
- Search history
- Filter preferences

**On Our Servers**:
- Anonymous usage statistics
- Error logs
- Performance metrics
- Feature usage data

### What We Don't Collect

**We Never Collect**:
- Personal information (name, email, phone)
- Financial information
- Cryptocurrency wallet addresses
- Passwords or secrets
- Location data
- Browsing history
- Tracking cookies
